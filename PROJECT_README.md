# xevoΣ PREMIUM Custom Residence — Photorealistic Digital Twin

Blender + Unreal Engine 5 realtime architectural digital twin of a Daiwa House
xevoΣ PREMIUM custom residence, with a **grand piano soundproof music room** as
the governing requirement.

---

## ⚠️ Read this first: the drawing set never arrived

The brief supplies a house to model from an attached set of 配置図 / 平面図 /
立面図 / 断面図. **No drawing files reached this session** — verified against the
attachment mount, the working directory, and a filesystem search
(see [`docs/DRAWING_ANALYSIS.md`](docs/DRAWING_ANALYSIS.md) §1).

The brief is explicit about what to do in that situation:

> いきなりモデリングを開始しない。
> 図面から確認できない寸法を勝手に確定値として扱ってはならない。

So no house has been modelled. A photorealistic walkthrough of an invented
building is the worst possible outcome for a project whose entire purpose is to
let someone know what their house will feel like before it is built — because
it would be believed.

**What was built instead is everything that does not depend on the drawings**,
including the full analysis of the CRITICAL music-room requirement, which turns
out to be answerable without them.

## The findings worth acting on now

From [`docs/PIANO_FIT_REPORT.md`](docs/PIANO_FIT_REPORT.md), generated by an
executable solver, not by assertion:

| # | Finding |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **The acoustic lining costs ~11 % of the music room's floor** — about 2 帖 of the 17.3 帖. Two grands still fit, via the nested duo layout. Floor area is *not* the binding constraint. |
| 2 | **Volume is the binding constraint.** Every candidate shape at every plausible ceiling height falls short of the ~76 m³ guidance for two grand pianos. Best case tested: 68.4 m³ at a 3.00 m gross ceiling. |
| 3 | **At a 2.4 m gross ceiling the lined room is 2.05 m — below the 2.1 m minimum in 建築基準法施行令第21条.** The build-up has to be checked against the section before the 確認申請. |
| 4 | **A typical single-leaf 防音ドア (750 mm clear) cannot admit a grand piano** (800 mm practical minimum). Acoustic doors are *narrower* than ordinary internal doors. Invisible on a plan, expensive on delivery day. |
| 5 | **One room proportion is clearly best:** 4.550 × 6.370 m gross — largest net floor *and* the fewest degenerate axial modes of the shapes tested. If the proportion is still open with Daiwa House, ask for this one. |

Items 2–4 are cheap to fix on the drawing board and expensive to fix on site.
They are worth raising **before** the drawings are finalised.

## Running it

Everything runs on stock Python 3 with **no dependencies**.

```bash
python3 scripts/acoustic_piano_fit.py       # regenerate the piano study (~2-3 min)
python3 scripts/qa_verify.py --markdown     # run the QA gate, refresh the checklist
python3 scripts/build_house.py --dry-run    # validate parameters; refuses to guess
python3 Unreal/Python/setup_cameras.py      # place QA cameras (4/19 placeable today)
```

The QA gate is the honest status of the project:

```
PASS 5   FAIL 0   BLOCKED 17   WARN 1

⏸  3 CRITICAL check(s) BLOCKED on the drawing set.
   Not a failure — but not a pass either.
```

`BLOCKED` is deliberately not `PASS`. A check whose input has never been
supplied has not been satisfied. Conflating the two is how the music room went
missing in earlier attempts at this model.

## What unblocks the most

In descending order of value — **the 1階平面図 alone unblocks six items and the
single most valuable unknown in the project** (the music room's aspect ratio):

1. **1階平面図** — footprint, GFA, room layout, **music room shape**
2. **断面図** — floor and ceiling heights, **music room ceiling**, void, stair
3. **立面図** — roof, eaves, openings
4. **2階平面図** — void outline, balcony, bedrooms
5. **配置図** — site, true north, setbacks

DXF/DWG is best (mechanical extraction), PDF next, legible photographs usable.

## Non-negotiable

The 1F ~17.3 帖 space **is** a GRAND PIANO SOUNDPROOF MUSIC ROOM with two grand
pianos playable by two people at once. It is not a bedroom, a spare room, a
guest room, a plain western room, or a multi-purpose room.

This has been lost before. It is now enforced by `scripts/qa_verify.py`, which
fails the build rather than shipping without it — see
[`docs/PIANO_ROOM_REQUIREMENTS.md`](docs/PIANO_ROOM_REQUIREMENTS.md).
